How It Works

The Circular Process

→
Step 01

Maize Shelling

Our industrial-grade 7.5 HP maize sheller efficiently separates well-dried maize kernels from cobs. This process is crucial in reducing post-harvest losses that often devastate smallholder farmers.

Step 02

Feed Milling

Our versatile 7.5 HP multipurpose feed chopper transforms agricultural waste into valuable livestock feed. It shreds maize stalks, grinds cobs, and processes grains into nutritious, affordable animal feed.

Our Mission

Building Climate Resilience, One Community at a Time

We target smallholder farmers, farmer groups, and local markets across Kenya. By providing affordable maize shelling and locally milled feed, we create a circular economy that benefits the entire agricultural value chain.

Our approach reduces dependence on expensive imported feeds, minimizes post-harvest waste, and empowers communities to build sustainable, climate-resilient livelihoods.
