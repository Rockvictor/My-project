7.5 HP Maize Sheller

Maize Shelling

Our industrial-grade 7.5 HP sheller efficiently separates well-dried maize kernels from cobs. This process is crucial in reducing post-harvest losses that often devastate smallholder farmers.

  • High-throughput processing for large and small batches
  • Clean separation of kernels from cobs
  • Preserves grain quality for storage and market sale
  • Significantly reduces manual labor time

7.5 HP Multipurpose Feed Chopper

Feed Milling

Our versatile 7.5 HP multipurpose feed chopper transforms agricultural waste into valuable livestock feed. It shreds maize stalks, grinds cobs, and processes grains to produce nutritious, affordable animal feed.

  • Shreds maize stalks for roughage feed
  • Grinds cobs and grains into fine meal
  • Produces feeds for poultry, dairy, pigs, and goats
  • Upcycles waste into high-value nutrition
